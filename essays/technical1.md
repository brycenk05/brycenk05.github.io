## When did it start?

My interest in technology began at a young age through video games on systems such as the Nintendo Wii, Nintendo DS, Xbox 360, and even mobile devices. While I initially enjoyed them simply as a form of entertainment, I became increasingly curious about the technology behind them and how user inputs could be translated into the interactive experiences displayed on screen. This curiosity stayed with me throughout high school and gradually developed into a broader interest in the relationship between people and technology. As my interest grew, I began to shift my academic focus from primarily working with people to exploring how technology can be designed to support, enhance, and benefit human interaction. This change in perspective ultimately led me to pursue studies in Information and Computer Sciences at the University of Hawaii at Manoa, where I began developing the technical knowledge and problem-solving skills that form the foundation of software engineering.

With a fresh start at the university level, I initially found myself navigating unfamiliar territory, but through each course I gradually developed both confidence and technical skills. Working with programming languages such as Java, C, and C++, I learned how to approach problems logically and create solutions through code. One project that strengthened my interest in software engineering was designing a banking user interface in my ICS 212 coursework, which introduced me to the importance of creating software that is both functional and user-friendly. Through this experience, I learned that successful software development involves more than writing code and it also requires understanding the needs of users and designing systems that are intuitive and accessible. These experiences have also encouraged me to explore related fields such as cybersecurity and linguistics, both of which demonstrate how technology, communication, and human interaction are closely connected.

## How will it end?

As I continue my studies, I hope to further develop my programming, software design, and problem-solving abilities through both coursework and hands-on experiences. The same curiosity that first drew me toward technology continues to motivate me to learn more about how software can be used to address real-world challenges. I am particularly interested in exploring cybersecurity and linguistics, as they represent different ways technology can impact people's lives through security, communication, and accessibility. Through future internships, collaborative projects, and professional experiences, I hope to gain a deeper understanding of software development practices while strengthening my ability to work effectively with others. By continuing to build both technical and interpersonal skills, I aim to become a well-rounded professional capable of contributing to meaningful and impactful technology solutions.
