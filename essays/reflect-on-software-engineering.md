## Introduction
Jumping into ICS 314, I had a rough idea of what concepts and coursework to expect from the syllabus. However, I didn't fully understand was how those different aspects would come together throughout my time in this course. Slowly but surely, I began to see that developing software involves not only creating a working application, but also determining how a team should approach its work, how software should be structured, and what responsibilities developers have toward the people who use it. Concepts such as agile project management, design patterns, and ethics in software engineering particularly helped me develop this broader understanding. In this essay I plan to go more in depth about these concepts and how they not only applied to web development, but how they extend to software engineering as a whole.
