Traveled to Kumejima in Okinawa, Japan during Winter 2025 with fellow participants from Waiakea High School. 

During the trip, I was tasked with recording my findings and sharing them with those involved with arranging the exchange. The main initiative of the program was to strengthen the relationship between Japan and the US, and for this first half, I would notice the similarities, specifically the strong connection with agriculture found in Kumejima and the Big Island. There was a large reliance on the ecosystem and the environment was very rural compared to the city-like feeling of Tokyo.

In a similar sense, Kumejima was comparable to Tokyo like how the Big Island was comparable to Oahu. For instance, the population difference between each pairs were noticeable as well as how less work driven Kumejima and the Big Island were compared to their more city-like counterparts.

<img class="img-fluid" src="../img/exchange-body2">

For the 2nd half, I was also assigned two Japanese exchange students from Kumejima to show around the Big Island, where the also noted how similar it was to their home. At the end of everything, I would write a short essay about the importance of the US and Japan's relationship.
