## What is this project?

Outdoor Buddies aims to help students find others interested in hiking, running, and walking around Oahu. The application will allow users to create profiles, discover compatible groups and activities, join outdoor events, and share information about local outdoor locations.

The goal is to make outdoor activities more accessible, social, and safer by helping students connect with others who share similar interests and preferences.

## How did I contribute?

With just two people, we were each assigned a multitude of tasks, similar to one another. On the face of the database, I developed the announcements page, which served as a way for admins and/or developers to interact with users across the site in the form of announcements only they can make. Another page I worked heavily on were the Hikes tab, which served as a database for users to discover what hike they would like to explore next. Featured on the hikes page was a search bar and filter that worked hand-in-hand. Other parts I worked on were schemas for each table, and the add and edit forms, which were altered by me and my teammate according to what we needed. Finally I do believe my biggest contribution in the background was definitely the playwright testing to ensure that every possible feature functioned properly on the website, this ranged from add, edit delete functions, and strict user access to pages.

## What did I gain from this project?

Having only recently learned the skills utilized in this project, I was able to cement my role as a developer and a collaborator. In terms of social and teamwork skills, I strengthened my ability to communicate vital information with my partner and meet in a timely manner. While we did have bumps in the road, I kept my composure and was able to persevere through any issues which displayed my growth as a developer. Having only worked on personal projects in the past, this project taught me to meet deadlines and in turn, improve my ability to write cleaner, easier to read code that functioned unlike how I'm used to with languages such as Java or C++. 

If you are interested in more details, you can find our developer home page for our project [here](https://outdoor-buddies.github.io/)
